05 / KEDI + JEVJEV BY TYPESAFE

Typed judgments.
Your next move.

Pick a queue. Assess cancellation intent. Let your program decide the priority.

Choice
A Literal limits the answer to your defined options.
Probability
A value from 0 to 1. Keep the assessment; set your own decision rule.

06 / KEDI NOTEBOOKA LITTLE ROOM TO EXPERIMENT.

One cell leads to another.

Try a template. Inspect its values. Keep going in the next cell, with your program's state right where you left it.

$kedi notebook

Opens your browser at localhost:8788/notebook/. Served locally, on your machine.

First-time setup

From a Kedi source checkout with its submodules initialized, install the notebook dependencies and launch it with:

uv run --extra notebook kedi notebook

After installation, run kedi notebook with that environment active.

Your Python, your choice.

Start with browser Python, or select a host interpreter with a reusable notebook virtual environment.

More than a scratchpad.

Mix Kedi, Markdown, and terminal cells. Save a .kedinb notebook, with optional execution progress and no Secret Manager values.
